# SuperSEO & Quality Standards (InhouseSEO Framework)

Permanent rules and guidelines adopted from `https://github.com/inhouseseo/superseo-skills`:

## 1. Anti-AI-Slop Writing Standards
- **Eliminate Tier 1 Banned AI Vocabulary**: 
  - Never use: *delve, tapestry, realm, landscape (metaphorical), multifaceted, nuanced, testament, cutting-edge, revolutionary, comprehensive (as generic filler), crucial, compelling, vibrant, game-changer, leverage, unlock potential, harness, endeavour, embark, navigate, pivotal, intricate, innovative, seamless, robust, transformative, meticulous, facilitate, utilize, plethora, myriad, underscore, bolster, spearhead.*
  - Never use AI intro/outro transitions: *"In today's fast-paced world...", "It's worth noting that...", "Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally" (as lazy sentence starters), "Without further ado", "In conclusion / In summary".*
- **Sentence Burstiness & Rhythm**:
  - Vary sentence length aggressively: mix short punchy sentences (3-7 words) with complex, explanatory clauses (25-35 words). Avoid uniform AI cadence.
- **The Horoscope Test**:
  - Every paragraph must make concrete, falsifiable claims. If a sentence could be applied to any competitor or any industry simply by swapping the name, rewrite it with specific numbers, tools, litology names, or field metrics.

## 2. Google Information Gain Standard (Patent June 2024 & API Leak)
- **OriginalContentScore Requirement**:
  - Never generate boilerplate templates where only 5% of variables change (e.g. city name swaps).
  - Every commercial or local page must include **first-party proprietary value**:
    1. Real local project case study / client outcome.
    2. Specific local geological/litological challenge (e.g. formation hardness, water salinity, confining bed depth).
    3. Operational reality (mobilization base, crew logistics, real local pricing).
  - Merely wrapping public domain data (such as ESDM geology maps) in an identical 95% sales template is classified by Google as **Doorway Pages (Scaled Content Abuse)**.

## 3. Topical Authority & Semantic Architecture (Koray Tuğberk & Kyle Roof POP)
- Structure content in strict Hub-and-Spoke clusters:
  - **Pillar/Hub**: Core high-level guide establishing topical entity.
  - **Spoke**: Dedicated deep dives answering specific high-intent problems.
- Maintain primary keyword in Title, H1, first 100 words, and URL slug. Do NOT keyword-stuff body paragraphs.
- Keep sitemaps clean, compact, and free of low-quality doorway URLs.
